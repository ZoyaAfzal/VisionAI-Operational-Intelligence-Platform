## 1. Context- what VisionAI is

VisionAI is a Video Management System (VMS) layered with AI computer-vision analytics for multi-location businesses (restaurant chains, mall operators, retail chains, warehouses). It connects to CCTV/DVR camera networks at each physical location and runs AI models on the video feeds to detect operational events- long queues, dirty tables, safety hazards, compliance violations- turning them into real-time alerts, dashboards, and automated workflows for area/regional managers and admins.

Core domain concepts (preserve exactly, they're already reflected in the existing UI):

- **Locations**- physical sites (restaurant, mall, warehouse, office, store, airport kiosk, distribution center), each with a manager, region, status, and health score. Existing seeded locations: Downtown Restaurant, Mall Food Court, Main Street Store, Airport Kiosk.
- **Cameras**- CCTV devices per location, tied to a DVR/NVR (Hikvision, Axis, Dahua, Hanwha, Bosch, Uniview), with Active / Inactive / Disabled status. Inventory, Live grid, and single-camera Monitoring detail are three distinct views of the same camera set.
- **AI Workflows**- pre-built or custom detection rules ("Dirty Table Detection," "Queue Management," "Wet Floor Safety Monitor") that watch camera feeds and generate Alerts. Installable from a Marketplace or built via a natural-language Workflow Builder.
- **Alerts**- events generated by AI Workflows (Critical / Warning / Info severity), with a lifecycle (New → In Progress → Resolved), assignable to staff, each linked to a video clip (see the "Alert Clip" modal with real-time queue analytics overlay).
- **Analytics**- footfall, queue time, compliance score, location health, trends over time (line/bar charts + location comparison bars).
- **Compliance / Reports / Integrations / Settings**- supporting admin sections.

## 3. Pages & required functionality

Build/complete each of the following as full, working routes- no "coming soon" placeholders unless explicitly noted:

1. **Overview**- KPI cards (Footfall Today, Active Alerts, Compliance Score, Avg. Queue Time, Location Health), each with a trend sparkline and % change badge. Recent alerts table below.
2. **Alerts**- Full alert management table: Time, Location, Alert Type, Severity badge, Status (New/In Progress/Resolved, color-coded), Camera, and row actions (View Video → opens the Alert Clip modal with bounding-box overlay + real-time queue analytics panel, Assign, Resolve). Filters: severity, date range, location.
3. **AI Workflows**- List of installed workflows with enabled toggle and a star rating, a Marketplace to install new templates, and a natural-language Workflow Builder entry point.
4. **Analytics**- Footfall Trend and Queue Time Avg line charts, Compliance Violations bar chart (by day of week), Location Comparison horizontal bar chart.
5. **Locations**- Grid/list of all locations with manager, region, status, and health score; drill-in to a location detail view.
6. **Cameras**
   - **Inventory** tab- full camera list per location with DVR/NVR vendor, status.
   - **Live** tab- grid of live feed cards for a selected location; Active cameras show the feed + bounding boxes + per-camera label + timestamp; Inactive/Disabled cameras show a dimmed placeholder card with a camera-off icon and "No live feed available."
   - **Monitoring** tab- single-camera detail view: large feed, bounding-box overlays with per-person tracking IDs and wait times, a floating Queue Analytics panel (Queue Length, Average Wait Time, Predicted Wait), "Extended Wait Detected" warning badges, Active/Inactive status pill, and a "Back to Inventory" link.
7. **Reports**- generated/schedulable reports (can remain a lighter-weight section, but must be a real functioning page, not a stub).
8. **Compliance**- compliance score breakdown and violation history.
9. **Integrations**- DVR/NVR and third-party integration connections.
10. **Settings**- account, notification, and org-level settings.

Global chrome on every page: left sidebar nav (Overview, Alerts with unread-count badge, AI Workflows, Analytics, Locations, Cameras, Reports, Compliance, Integrations, Settings), top bar (search, theme toggle, notification bell with count, location switcher dropdown defaulting to "All Locations", user menu), and a small system-status + mobile-app card pinned at the bottom of the sidebar.
